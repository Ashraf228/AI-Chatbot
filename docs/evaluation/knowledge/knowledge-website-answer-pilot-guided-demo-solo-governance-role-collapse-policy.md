## Policy Rule 1: Solo Operator Context Boundary

- A future same-person exception is relevant only in a genuine solo-founder or solo-operator context where role separation is operationally unavailable.
- Convenience, speed, or engineering ownership alone are insufficient to justify role collapse.
- This task does not determine whether any real future operator meets that context.

## Policy Rule 2: Conditional Same-Person Role Collapse Boundary

- The policy may later allow the same person to hold both named-owner and final-approver roles only as a bounded exception.
- The exception is conditional, not default, and not self-activating.
- The policy does not allow same-person assignment without the later artifacts listed below.

## Policy Rule 3: Named Owner / Final Approver Separation Preference

- Distinct named-owner and final-approver roles remain the preferred default.
- Same-person role collapse is a fallback exception only when separate real humans are not reasonably available.
- If separation is feasible, separation remains required.

## Policy Rule 4: Off-Repo Identity Reference Boundary

- Any future same-person exception requires a separate off-repo identity reference.
- The repository must not silently become the system of record for personal identity details.
- This task creates no off-repo identity reference.

## Policy Rule 5: PII-In-Repo Prohibition Boundary

- Names, email addresses, phone numbers, and other contact data remain out of scope for this repository unless separately approved in a secure path.
- This policy records no real person and no PII.
- The policy does not weaken the no-PII-in-repo rule.
